# Design vault

Maintain concise Markdown with YAML frontmatter and resolvable wikilinks. `Home.md` is the
entry point. Distinguish accepted design, current implementation, measured evidence and
pending tests. Give source dates/builds for compatibility claims; retain uncertainty.

Update the implementation ledger with concrete validation evidence. Do not label a plan or
passing mock test as a successful in-game playtest. Keep credentials and raw player secrets
out of the vault. No Obsidian community plugin is required to read the project.
